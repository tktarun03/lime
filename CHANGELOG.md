# Changelog

Newest first. Every release also updates `LIME.RELEASES` in `js/core/version.js`,
the `?v=` on every asset link in `index.html`, `VERSION` in `sw.js`, and
`version` in `package.json`. `tests/structure.test.mjs` fails if they differ.

## 0.7.1 — 2026-11-09

- Pinch and double-tap zoom are off, so the app feels like a native app. Use
  Settings → Look → Text size for bigger text.
- The menu bar is now fixed to the bottom edge and also shows while editing
  your plan.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-09

- Man photos for all 35 exercises. Choose Woman or Man in Settings → Look.
- New LIME logo on the Home Screen icon, browser tab and start screen.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-11-09

- Choose whether exercise photos show a woman or a man, at sign-up or in
  Settings → Look.
- Man photos are coming in batches; until then those moves show the woman.

## 0.5.1 — 2026-11-09

- Fixed: if you had turned off 3D demos in an older version, exercise photos
  were hidden. Photos now show by default.
- Self-check now says when photos are hidden by the Exercise demos setting.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-08

- Edit your plan: add, swap, remove and reorder exercises, change sets, reps
  and rest, rename days, add or delete days.
- Weight suggestions: go up when you hit every rep, stay or go lighter when
  you don't. Turn off in Settings.
- Changing your profile no longer overwrites a plan you edited.

## 0.4.4 — 2026-11-08

- Every exercise photo now shows the same athlete: new photos for Russian
  twist, mountain climber, high knees, hamstring stretch and hip flexor stretch.

## 0.4.3 — 2026-11-08

- New Refresh button on Home and in Settings: reloads the app and gets the
  latest version. Your data stays.

## 0.4.2 — 2026-11-08

- New photos for back squat, bench press, overhead press, calf raise and
  superman, with the same athlete as the other moves.

## 0.4.1 — 2026-11-08

- Five exercises renamed and rewritten to match their photos: Tempo Squat
  (was Jump Squat), Dumbbell Deadlift (was Barbell Deadlift), Seated Dumbbell
  Press, One-Arm Dumbbell Row and Seated Band Row.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-08

- Every exercise now shows real photos of the start and end position.
- Choose Photos, 3D or Simple drawings in Settings → Look → Exercise demos.
- Library thumbnails use the photos too.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-07

- More lifelike 3D bodies: shaped muscles, real lighting and reflections.
- Frosted-glass look with a floating tab bar.
- Smaller text overall, plus Auto (your phone's size), S, M, L and XL in Settings.
- Goblet squat now holds the weight at the chest.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-07

- Exercise demos are now 3D bodies, with the worked muscles lit up.
- Thinner, lighter type throughout.
- Goblet squat shows one weight held at the chest.
- Turn 3D demos off in Settings to save battery.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-07

- First version: profile set-up, and a plan built from your goal, days and equipment.
- Workout player with sets, rest timer, 3-2-1 countdown and screen kept on.
- Exercise library with moving illustrations, steps and common mistakes.
- Progress: weekly volume, streak, personal records, body weight and a 12-week calendar.
- Backup and restore to a file. Everything stays on your phone.
