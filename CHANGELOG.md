# Changelog

Newest first. Every release also updates `LIME.RELEASES` in `js/core/version.js`,
the `?v=` on every asset link in `index.html`, `VERSION` in `sw.js`, and
`version` in `package.json`. `tests/structure.test.mjs` fails if they differ.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-07

- First version: profile set-up, and a plan built from your goal, days and equipment.
- Workout player with sets, rest timer, 3-2-1 countdown and screen kept on.
- Exercise library with moving illustrations, steps and common mistakes.
- Progress: weekly volume, streak, personal records, body weight and a 12-week calendar.
- Backup and restore to a file. Everything stays on your phone.
